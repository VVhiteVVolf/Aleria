Charakter-Emotionssheet Master Prompt (9er Set)
Take the provided portrait image as the ONLY reference for the character.
Create a complete 3×3 expression and gesture sheet (9 panels) featuring the EXACT SAME character.
Identity Preservation (Highest Priority)
The character must remain 100% identical in every panel.
Preserve exactly:
Face shape and proportions
Eyes, eyebrows and nose
Hairstyle, hair volume and hairline
Facial hair
Armor, clothing, colors and accessories
Age
Art style
Line quality
Rendering quality
Lighting
Color palette
Do NOT redesign, reinterpret or stylize the character differently.
Every panel should feel like the same portrait with altered expressions and poses, not nine separate illustrations.

Background Consistency
Use the same set-appropriate background in every panel. Default: reference portrait. Sets 24/25: the same cozy room and table.
Same environment
Same lighting
Same camera distance
Same perspective
No simplification
No redesign
The only changes should be the character’s expression, head angle (minor), gaze and body gesture.

Composition
Square overall format (1:1)
3×3 grid
Nine equally sized panels
No white borders
No text
No captions
No symbols
No speech bubbles
Only the selected set's named props; otherwise hands free. Sets 24/25 allow their dining props.
Square chest-up framing matching the approved examples. Keep the camera at face height. If needed, zoom out 10–15% from the previous set-specific crop to give hands, forearms and gestures more room. Keep the face and upper body prominent, with compact gestures near the chest. Use the same chosen framing across all nine panels and retain each set's specific crop limits. Elbows and lower forearms may naturally leave the frame.
Same camera angle and framing across all panels
Minimal variation in rotation only when it supports the expression

Expression Consistency
This is NOT nine different drawings.
It must look like the same portrait whose facial muscles and body language change naturally.
Changes should primarily affect:
Eyebrows
Eyes
Eyelids
Mouth
Jaw tension
Head tilt
Eye direction
Avoid changing:
Skull proportions
Hair shape
Armor proportions
Clothing folds unnecessarily
High character consistency is critical.

Gesture Rules
Every emotion must also be expressed through:
Hands
Arms
Shoulders
Head position
Torso posture
Eye direction
Facial expression and compact hand gestures work together within the fixed chest-up crop.
Every panel should immediately communicate a usable RPG reaction.
Avoid neutral filler poses.
Every tile should have a clear storytelling purpose.
No repeated gestures.

Quadratisches Brustbild wie in den freigegebenen Rianna- und Lefraye-Beispielen. Die Kamera bleibt auf Gesichtshöhe. Bei Bedarf darf sie 10–15 % weiter herauszoomen; die untere Bildkante darf entsprechend etwas tiefer liegen. Kopf, Gesicht, Schultern und Brust bleiben Schwerpunkt. Hände und Gesten bleiben kompakt in Brustnähe; Ellbogen und tiefere Unterarme dürfen am Bildrand angeschnitten sein. Keine Beine oder Ganzkörperansicht. In allen neun Panels denselben gewählten Ausschnitt verwenden.
Set 4 verwendet ruhige, kleine, natürliche Handgesten und zurückhaltende Mimik. Brauen, Mundwinkel, Blick und Kopfneigung verändern sich nur leicht. Die Hände bleiben nahe am Brustbereich. Die Körperhaltung bleibt entspannt und fast aufrecht.
