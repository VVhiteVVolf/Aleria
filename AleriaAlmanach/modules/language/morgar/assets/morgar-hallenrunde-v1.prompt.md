## Verwendeter Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a finished illustration for the opening page of the fictional Morgar language module in the Aleria fantasy almanac. Portrait image, exact aspect ratio 2:3. One cohesive medieval mountain-hall scene showing the language of the sturdy, broad-shouldered dwarflike Morgorner being used in everyday and feudal life: a commanding stout female liege in a simple iron circlet and heavy crimson cloak, a hugely broad grinning bearded male warrior, and a stocky female smith in a leather apron animatedly talking around a worn wooden hall table with bread, a wooden tankard and a rolled parchment. Clearly distinguish the three main figures through silhouettes, faces and expressive gestures. Emphasize lively communication, fellowship and rugged authority. Background: massive stone pillars, timber beams, geometric hanging banners, a small orange forge glow deeper in the hall, a cool blue view of mountains through an arch. Show enough environment to make this a scene rather than a portrait lineup, use a slightly low viewpoint and strong foreground/middle/background staging. Characters and expressions dominate; all are original fantasy characters. Strict One Piece anime art style: bold exaggerated faces and body proportions, strong expressive eyebrows and mouths, clean decisive anime lineart, flat color areas and crisp clean cel-shading. Soft simple anime surface textures for skin, cloth, wood and stone. No realistic surface detail, no photorealism, no painterly rendering, no 3D rendering. Warm hearth-orange and deep earthy colors balanced with cool mountain blue. Vertical composition with the main faces comfortably inside the frame. No text, no lettering, no speech bubbles, no logo, no watermark, no border.
