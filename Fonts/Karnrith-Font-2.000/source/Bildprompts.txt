## Ergaenzung-16.png

## Tastatur-16.png

Use the attached KARNRITH II TIEFENRUNEN image as a strict visual STYLE reference. Create a raster font master sheet of exactly SIXTEEN recognizable keyboard characters in 4 rows and 4 columns. They MUST still be easily recognizable as the specified conventional digits and punctuation, but redesigned in the same heavy tall architectural DWARVEN script. No invented replacement symbols on this sheet.
Reading order:
Row 1: 0 1 2 3
Row 2: 4 5 6 7
Row 3: 8 9 & @
Row 4: # $ € §
NO title, NO labels under cells, NO borders, NO other text. Pale plain ivory background. Each character a solid near-black filled glyph, identical cap height and generous whitespace inside its cell. Exact equal 4 by 4 grid, square canvas.
Style: bold vertical pillars, blunt wedge serifs, chamfered angular bowl corners instead of smooth circles, diagonal-cut crossbars, large open readable counters, a little broad-chisel calligraphic variation. Tall sturdy proportions about 1.5 to 1.8 times taller than wide. Match attached Tiefenrunen weight and confident severe rhythm. The zero has a clearly hollow chamfered oval body; 1 distinct from lowercase L; 6 and 9 distinct; & and @ remain recognizable; € has its two center bars. Enough interior whitespace for real webfont rendering. Slight handmade outline irregularity, no distress holes, no cracks, no decorative background. NOT thin one-line SVG runes, NOT Latin blackletter copy, no metallic gradients, no 3D lighting. This is an actual usable number and punctuation typeface design sheet.
