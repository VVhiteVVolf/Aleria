# nimuiten-nimue-vision-v1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: main portrait of the Nimuiten sect on a medieval religion codex website. Reference image is STYLE AND NIMUE APPEARANCE REFERENCE: green-blue water goddess, golden cup, Gothic sacred glass. New artwork, vertical 1024 × 1536, strict 2:3. An unsettling, much more abstract devotional vision of Nimue as imagined by waiting followers. Her elongated serene face and long turquoise hair emerge indistinctly from layered moonlit sea currents, the lower body dissolves into broad abstract waves and a narrow distant horizon. A small empty golden chalice hangs in the foreground; her hands are only hinted at by flowing shapes. A remote drowned kingdom is suggested by a few ambiguous spire shapes beneath glassy water, an image of longing rather than a confirmed place. Frontal sacred composition, pointed Gothic stained-glass window silhouette, simplified lead divisions, deep midnight teal, faded sea green and old gold. Asymmetric shadows veil the eyes and lend an uncanny quiet presence without making her malicious. Flat stylized illustrated glass, not a photograph of a real window, not photorealistic. Her true character is compassionate; this is a followers imagined vision, NOT a demon goddess. No second deity, no devil horns, no tentacles, no gore, no victims, no text, no lettering, no watermarks. Fill the vertical canvas with the artwork.
