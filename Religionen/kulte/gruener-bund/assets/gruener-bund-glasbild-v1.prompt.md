# gruener-bund-glasbild-v1

Erzeugt mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug. Bildformat 2:3.

Use case: stylized-concept. Portrait 2:3, 1024 x 1536. A single luminous medieval stained glass illustration for a fantasy religion codex. Bold black lead outlines, richly colored translucent emerald, jade, amber-gold, ivory and midnight-blue glass, intricate but clearly structured flat decorative shapes, gothic arched panel. An ancient living oak with intertwined roots fills the center; its leafy canopy shelters three tiny Gaelic thatched roundhouses, a clear spring and small silhouettes of a dove, a brown bear, a deer and a curled dragon. Nine amber star shapes among the leaves. It symbolizes a peaceful nature community's belief in an unbroken creation. No people. Strong heraldic hierarchy, symmetrical calm design, not anime, not oil painting, no photorealism. No text, no lettering, no captions, no watermark. Whole glass panel visible without crop.
