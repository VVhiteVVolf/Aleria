# gruener-bund-symbol-v1

Erzeugt mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug. Registersymbol.

Use case: stylized-concept. A single religious emblem for the fictional Green Covenant, a peaceful radical nature movement in a medieval fantasy world. Square icon. Match this visual style: flat matte muted antique gold and ochre with restrained forest-green accents, strong dark brown contours, simple heraldic silhouette, subtle fine decorative grain, illuminated medieval manuscript icon as in an old religion codex. A single old oak whose roots and branches meet to form an open circular wreath; nine simple leaves in its crown, roots clearly visible below. Warm ivory background #f3ead7, ample empty margin on all sides, centered, readable at small size. No letters, no words, no numerals, no ribbon, no photographic or shiny 3D texture, no people.
