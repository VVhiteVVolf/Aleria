## Acting Now, or Only Talking
Where the Guidelines above treat a hint, a suggestion or a first step toward an action as enough to choose it, this rule takes precedence. Choose an action only if Balimund does it now, in their latest line (under Most Recent Exchange): agreeing to a request, obeying an order, or deciding to act this moment. If the line or the intent only talks about it, choose None: a plan or promise for later, a maybe, a suggestion, a question, a joke, a story, getting ready ("prepares to", "readies", "is about to"), or a threat or warning of what will happen later or "if" something else does. Carrying a threat out in the line itself (the attack, the order, the verdict) is acting. So is agreeing now to a deal whose pickup or payment comes later (an order, a tab, a loan, a raise): the agreement is settled in this line. Starting an interrogation now is acting too, though it is done by asking. Money that Balimund chooses to move in that line is acting too, even if nobody asked: handing someone coin (a tip, a reward, settling up, a refund), billing Alawen for something just given or done (a charge on their tab, or collecting what they owe; they are asked to confirm). Billing anyone else still needs their agreement. Naming a price, haggling or saying they will pay later without agreeing a tab or loan now is talk.
Going somewhere together with Alawen is not a journey of Balimund's own: someone following Alawen right now simply comes along, and agreeing now to come along, or being called along after a wait, is joining, tagging along or following again, not setting off.
When unsure, choose None.
## Validate the Operation Before Selecting It
These checks take precedence over general instructions to act on hints, agreement, or willingness. Read the actual state and the Most Recent Exchange before choosing.
- For GiveItem or SellItem, identify an actually listed matching item and sufficient quantity in the speaker's inventory or merchant stock. Use its real name. If none matches, choose None in the required response format. Never manufacture an inventory name from the dialogue's description or silently substitute an unrelated item. This check does not prohibit a real quest action from issuing its scripted reward.
- An agreed-price sale is an Economy transaction. If only Items actions are available for that sale, choose None; GiveItem must not replace SellItem, bypass payment, or split the sale into a second gift.
- Use confirmed outcomes supplied in the context. Do not repeat a completed transfer or payment unless a genuinely new transaction was explicitly agreed. A failed operation may justify a corrected attempt after its stated obstacle is resolved. A spoken promise, claim, or queued request is not confirmation; missing outcome information is not proof of success.
- Output exactly one action in the required response format. If no eligible action can perform the grounded operation, choose None.
